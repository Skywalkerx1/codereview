What the breakdowns say:

- **The panel is what catches defects.** The deterministic layer
  alone recalls 0.458 and lets 62.5 % of defects escape at
  non-blocking severity; the panel takes recall to 1.000 and
  escapes to zero. That gap is the product's claim, now measured on
  the full public split.
- **Redundancy did its job exactly once per family.** Each family
  had precisely one defect it failed to match at blocking severity
  (openai: `secret-private-key`; nvidia: `ssrf-fetch`; deepseek:
  none) and in each case a sibling seat covered it, so no defect
  escaped the panel.
- **OpenAI was the precision seat** (0.852 any-source): fewest
  findings, nearly all on target. DeepSeek matched everything but
  at 0.500 precision; NVIDIA sat between on defects.
- **Clean controls are the weak point, and it is mostly NVIDIA.**
  3 of 5 clean cases drew findings; NVIDIA contributed to all
  three, including a gate-blocking major on `clean-add-timeout-retry`
  ("no backoff between retries") and two of the three majors on
  `clean-health-guard-fix`. OpenAI added one blocking major on the
  latter. Net: **2 of 5 clean changes would have been blocked by
  the gate on false positives.** DeepSeek raised no findings on any
  clean case.

## 4. Caveats (quote these with the numbers)

1. **This is not the `panel-gemini-nvidia` panel.** Gemini was
  quota-dead at preflight and never served. The baseline describes
  an OpenAI + NVIDIA + DeepSeek panel. A Gemini-inclusive baseline
  still awaits a quota reset.
2. **Operations had one family.** "Both lenses served by healthy
  families" holds; "two families per lens" holds only for
  correctness.
3. **Recall 1.000 comes with precision 0.209.** The panel reported
  115 findings to match 24 defects (91 unmatched). On defect cases
  the extra findings are noise a reviewer must wade through; on
  clean cases they twice rose to gate-blocking false positives
  (§3). The escaped-defect headline and the clean-case behaviour
  must be quoted together.
4. **Public split only:** 24 of the corpus's 31 defects, 5 of its 7
  clean controls. Holdout was not touched.
5. **Cost is declared, not measured.** The config follows the
  shipped panel convention (`cost_per_call_usd: 0`): the Gemini /
  NVIDIA seats run on free tiers, and the OpenAI / DeepSeek seats'
  per-call micro-costs are not metered by this harness, so the
  $0.00 cost line means "unmetered", not "free".
6. Corpus v0 is small and Python-heavy (see README); treat these
  numbers as a baseline to regress against, not as field accuracy.

plus the corpus ground truth — with **no model calls**. The official
scorer (`bench.score_case`) reproduces §1 exactly (recall 24/24,

### 6.1 What the 115 findings are

The 115 are **merged, gate-level findings** — verified: the
`findings` lists of the 27 stored per-case records sum to exactly
115, and §1's 0.209 is the one-to-one scorer over those merged
findings. Cross-seat duplicates had already been collapsed by the
gate's merge before scoring: only 4 findings carry more than one
source (120 source incidences over 115 findings). The raw per-seat
job outputs were **not** preserved (the records' `reviewer_reports`
carry provider/lens/status only), so per-seat figures below are
reconstructed from the merged findings' `sources` by subset
rescoring — the §3 method — not from raw seat output.

### 6.3 Precision, three ways

**Naive (official, one-to-one over merged findings): 0.209**
(24 matched / 115). The scorer's one-to-one assignment means that
when several findings satisfy the match predicate for one defect,
exactly one is credited and the rest count as false positives.

**Merged-finding precision: 0.727** (24 / 33), with **82 duplicate
matches removed** — stated in full so the adjustment is visible, not
a quiet flattering of the number. Under the benchmark's own match
predicate (same file, and line within ±3 or title-token Jaccard
≥ 0.3), 106 of the 115 findings match some labelled defect; crediting
each covered defect once removes 106 − 24 = 82. But the duplicate
pool decomposes as follows, and most of it is **not** the cross-seat
duplication §2.2 hypothesised (that accounts for the 4 multi-source
findings above):

| Duplicate class | Count |
|---|---|
| Also meet the token-overlap bar vs the defect (plausible same-issue restatements) | 17 |
| Match by line proximity only, title Jaccard below threshold | 65 |
| — of which zero title-token overlap with the defect at all | 18 |

Whether a line-proximate finding is "the same defect" is a semantic
judgement the predicate cannot make; deciding it is the §2.3
adjudication step, not this repair. **Quote 0.727 only with its
duplicate count and this decomposition attached** (the
`required_caveat` in the frontmatter does so).

**By severity tier** (only blocker + major findings cause BLOCKED):

| Tier | Findings | Naive precision | Merged precision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blocking (blocker + major) | 90 | 0.267 (24/90) | 0.828 (24/29; 61 duplicates removed) |
| Minor / nit | 25 | 0.000 (0/25) | 0.000 (21 of the 25 predicate-match a defect already credited at blocking tier) |
