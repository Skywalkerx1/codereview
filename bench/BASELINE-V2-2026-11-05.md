## 7. Caveats (quote these with the numbers — there are no numbers)

1. **Voided, not failed.** No threshold in §1 was measured. B1 is
   neither confirmed nor refuted by this document; the replay's
   public-split row for B1 (step 4) remains exploration evidence
   only.
2. **The environment is the finding.** Seat reliability, not the
   policy, is what this run measured: over six starts, the
   DeepSeek seat errored in every degraded case and the NVIDIA
   free tier refused three preflights. A confirmation that
   tolerates zero degraded cases needs a provider environment
   steadier than the one available in this window — or a future
   registration that says, in advance, how degradation is handled.
3. **The default stays strict.** Nothing here changes any
   product behaviour: `strict` remains the default gate profile,
   `balanced` remains available as implemented in step 6.
4. **Do not quote voided-attempt records.** Partial records from
   attempts 1–4 and 6 exist on disk for provenance. Any figure
   computed from them mixes panels, profiles of completion, and
   an unrepresentative case prefix; it is not a baseline number.
5. **Cost is declared, not measured.** The panel config declares
   `cost_per_call_usd: 0` (v1's convention): free-tier seats plus
   unmetered per-call micro-costs on the paid seats. Six starts
   consumed roughly 340 seat jobs in total without producing a
   measurement — the real cost of a zero-degradation-tolerance
   design in a flapping environment.
